# Research note Q-02

## Evidence

- `Beam.Client/Pages/FrequencyList.razor` renders the frequency list and creation form.
- `Beam.Client/Pages/RayInput.razor` and `RayItem.razor` show ray creation, prism toggling, and count display.
- `Beam.Client/Pages/User.razor` and `Shared/UserLayout.razor` reveal the profile and "See Myself" behavior.
- `Beam.Client/Pages/Settings.razor` confirms username editing and the animated beam display.

## Findings

- The app UI is intentionally lightweight and centered around a fast feed view rather than a sophisticated social-network design.
- The original experience relies on a single current user with a generated anonymous name and can be preserved via session-local or server-side identity state.
